## Reference decisions

- [Minecraft: Tranquil Towers](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/tranquil-towers)
  informs silhouette-first design: unequal roof heights and an identifiable
  tower, before small decoration. We did not copy the creators' build.
- [Minecraft: PixlPlex's palace](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/peruse-pixlplex-s-perfect-palace)
  informs projecting upper floors, visible supports and deliberate roof color.
  The actual atelier geometry is original.
- Existing downloadable houses were investigated. The
  [EcoSMP collection](https://www.planetminecraft.com/project/big-medieval-house-build-free-schematic-download-full-video-tutorial-medieval-cottagecore-fantasy-whimsical/)
  explicitly limits commercial use; the
  [JJuulie house](https://www.mccreations.net/maps/medieval_house_and_decorations)
  is personal-use-only without further permission. Neither was imported into
  promotional footage. An accessible download is not a recorded promotion license.

## Actual set

`tools/video/java/org/marshal/wesovideo/ShowcaseWorld.java` builds the disposable
world using native Minecraft commands/features. No external map, resource pack
or imaginary mod rendering. The scene is filming-only and never ships in a JAR.

The house uses stone masonry, stripped spruce framing, calcite panels and a
teal prismarine/copper roof. Its asymmetrical tower makes clipboard rotation
and reflection obvious. A chimney, deep roof overhangs, balcony supports,
shutters, flower boxes and furnished workrooms give useful close-up detail.

The ground has rolling rises, trees placed at their actual surface height,
an irregular shallow pond, a dock, flower beds and a curving gravel approach.
The foreground should lead toward the house rather than look like a flat test pad.

Hidden gold/copper foundation runs, bookshelves, workstations, lanterns and
water are genuinely present. Isolation can reveal materials and furnishings;
native partial models and block entities are represented, not staged as an
unrelated static picture. Selection bounds exclude most of the surrounding set.

## Camera and light

Establishing view: elevated three-quarter framing, roof and ground floor both
visible. Orbit by only a few blocks, with an eased start/stop. Use the mod's
actual boundary at its selected thickness, with grid/ruler hidden for the
beauty take. Show them separately during the measurement demonstration.

Core evidence is vanilla. Optional Complementary LOW lighting is reserved
for beauty shots: no depth of field, motion blur or heavy bloom. Keep material
color and overlay lines readable. No external shader is a mod requirement.
