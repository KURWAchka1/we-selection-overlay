## Scope and restraint

- Show outcomes, not a setting-by-setting manual. Compatibility/package details
  stay in the description; the spoken ending keeps the local/server distinction.
- Preview, isolation and bounds editing remain visibly different operations.
  Moving a selection never pretends to move its blocks.
- Do not sell exact random placements or unrestricted remote-server simulation.
- Do not promise a measured frame rate or immediate loading from offline footage.
- Keep the approved voice, natural English/Russian translation and continuous
  closing orbit. No new permanent feature labels or fabricated mod graphics.
- Use only test-world command execution, then verify a real WorldEdit undo
  restores every source-house block. Existing capture/provenance checks remain.
