# Spoken script and closing revision

Reviewed 2026-10-11 after the owner's full-video feedback. This is an editorial
guide for this mod, not a claim that a detector can certify human authorship.
Keep the approved af_heart voice and base pace. Synthetic narration remains
disclosed in the publication description and asset provenance.

## What the research supports

| Primary or professional source | Finding and decision for this video |
| --- | --- |
| [Tercon and Dobrovoljc: linguistic characteristics survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.05136) | Research often finds formal, impersonal and repetitive prose, but is concentrated in English and particular models. Review patterns across the whole script; do not blacklist ordinary words as proof of AI. |
| [El Attar et al.: cross-domain linguistic analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04177) | This preprint studies 284 features, 27 models and ten domains. Many indicators depend on context. A Minecraft demonstration needs its own editorial judgment, not a universal score. |
| [Jakesch et al.: human heuristics are flawed](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.07271) | Readers' intuitions about generated language can be misleading. Do not promise undetectability or introduce typos, invented memories or fake hesitation to imitate a person. |
| [Rewriting the Script: voice interaction research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.09992) | Written instructions need adapting to speech, rather than simply reading a document aloud. Redistribute detail between the picture, voice and description. |
| [Adobe Podcast: write for your voice](https://podcast.adobe.com/en/guides/how-to-write-for-your-voice) | Word choice, rhythm, perspective and delivery work together. Use natural contractions, read-through review and pauses; avoid an over-polished announcer persona. |
| [Voices: conversational reads](https://www.voices.com/blog/conversational-read/) | Working actors recommend speaking to an imagined listener, varied inflection and restrained pronunciation. Explain a visible action to another builder, not to a corporate audience. |
| [BBC Academy: writing for different platforms](https://downloads.bbc.co.uk/academy/youngreporter/Lesson%20Plans%20-%20Lesson%20Five%20-%20Transcript%20English.pdf) | Pictures can tell part of a video story. Let the actual replacement, isolation and movement do that work; do not narrate every setting or repeat every visible label. |
| [GOV.UK: plain-English principles](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/govuk-content-principles-conventions-and-research-background/govuk-content-principles-conventions-and-research-background) | Prefer concrete everyday language and active verbs. | [NN/g: product-specific generated writing](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/genai-write-for-the-web/) | Their usability findings identify excess length and jargon as obstacles. Borrow the concision principle, not the web-page formatting, for spoken narration. |
| [Adobe: synthetic-dialogue writing](https://helpx.adobe.com/firefly/web/work-with-audio-and-video/work-with-video/best-practices-for-writing-content-for-text-to-avatar.html) | Spelling, punctuation and spoken-out numbers affect delivery. Apply text-level pronunciation fixes; that product's controls are not automatically supported by Kokoro. |
| [Netflix: Russian timed text](https://partnerhelp.netflixstudios.com/hc/en-us/articles/215346638-Russian-Timed-Text-Style-Guide) | Match the original register and keep coherent phrase breaks. These are editorial inputs, not evidence of mod performance or a comprehensive
inventory of every possible AI-writing signal. Research about written text
cannot prove how a particular synthetic voice will sound to a listener.

## House style

- One builder showing another what happens on screen. Friendly, specific and
  unhurried, with no invented personal experience or exaggerated excitement.
- Connect actions: check the material, try a replacement, inspect the result,
  cancel or apply. Avoid turning every section into identical imperative lists.
- Use contractions where someone would actually say them. Mix short observations
  with longer explanations; do not manufacture variation with random filler.
- Remove grand claims, empty transitions, abstract praise and repetitive slogans.
  No "game-changing", "seamless", "unlock", "delve", or generic recap paragraphs.
  These are project copy preferences, not universal AI-detection rules.
- Never recite the owner's bug reports, abandoned experiments, rejected behavior
  or a list of things not implemented. Demonstrate the shipped capability.
- Keep consequential facts: random patterns are illustrative, previews leave
  real data unchanged, moving bounds does not move the build, and local/server
  availability differs. Detailed patch/API installation advice goes below the
  video; removing clutter must not introduce a misleading compatibility claim.
- Translate meaning and tone into Russian. Prefer "потяните за угол" to a literal
  software-style noun chain. Do not pad captions with extra explanations.
- Keep the approved voice, avoid fake breaths/"uh" inserts and random pitch or
  speed changes. Use grammatical phrasing, a short inter-cue pause, a slightly
  longer chapter pause and a clean final release. Owner audition remains needed.

## Changes made

| Earlier approach | Revised treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Spoken cut/paste exclusions and copy safeguards | Show the copy, rotate/flip and restoration. No absent-feature checklist. |
| Partial-model bug explanation and camera-follow correction | Explain the useful command and bounds operation instead of development history. |
| Ten-cue patch-by-patch installation ending | Four short cues: preview status, local/server scope, links and a natural close. Exact versions remain in the description. |
| Repeated short final orbits restarting at each line | One separately recorded continuous native orbit. Each cue consumes the next frames, with no restart, loop or freeze padding. |
| Same short pause everywhere | Preserve the approved speaking speed; distinguish a connected sentence, a chapter change and the final tail. |

## Review gates

Script validation and timing checks are not an audio audition. Verify the actual
render: pronunciation of keys and product names, no clipped consonants or choppy
joins, captions synchronized with the action, a full six-second opening reveal,
and uninterrupted camera movement through every closing cue. Fade only after
the final words and captions have finished. Human approval and publication stay
pending until the owner has reviewed the revised cut.
